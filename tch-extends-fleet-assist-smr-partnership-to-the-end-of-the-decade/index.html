TCH extends Fleet Assist SMR partnership to the end of the decade

Monday 16th February 2026

TCH Leasing has renewed its SMR contract with Fleet Assist until the end of the decade across its fleet of nearly 8,000 vehicles to utilise its network of over 6,500 service points and 600 mobile units.

TCH uses Fleet Assist’s managed garage service network to ensure a seamless coordination of maintenance and repairs across its nationwide network of approved garages.

The renewal also covers Fleet Assist’s service booking, which streamlines the scheduling process for company vehicles, improving efficiency and vehicle uptime.

TCH provides a full range of services to the fleet market including contract hire, fleet management and salary sacrifice and prides itself on providing a complete after sales care package to every customer and its drivers.

Chris Nightingale, managing director at TCH Leasing said “Fleet Assist continues to set the bar in terms of SMR network garage management excellence. When combined with its market-leading business intelligence linked to the Atlas work direction engine it delivers a powerful combination.”

“The Fleet Assist team is very proactive in responding to market forces and helping us future proof our SMR provision, particularly around EVs. Using Fleet Assist’s work direction ensures we deliver the highest customer service across the UK whilst effectively managing our SMR spend, it is an important part of our customer proposition,” he added.

TCH believes Fleet Assist provides them with the logical choice, driven by data and technology underpinned by a highly experienced team with a laser focus on detail to deliver a “best in class SMR services.

“We are delighted TCH has renewed its contract, and this is testament to our ability to evolve to meet the ever-changing SMR demands of fleet. We are proud to support TCH with a truly, flexible, customer first solution,” said Vincent St Claire, Fleet Assist’s MD.
